# Code of Conduct

We welcome contributors with different backgrounds and levels of experience.
Help keep the project a place where people can ask questions, learn, and disagree
constructively.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient, and specific in feedback.
- Critique ideas and code, not the person proposing them.
- Respect privacy, intellectual property, and boundaries.
- Give credit and make space for people who are new to the project.

Harassment, discriminatory language, threats, unwanted sexual attention,
doxxing, and deliberate disruption are not acceptable.

## Scope and reporting

These expectations apply to repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and
other project-managed spaces, and when representing the project publicly.
Report concerns privately to **wondev.support@gmail.com**. Provide context
without sharing unnecessary personal data.

Maintainers will review reports, protect the reporter's privacy where possible,
and choose a proportionate response. Responses may include asking for a change,
removing content, restricting participation, or banning repeat or severe offenders.
Reports involving a maintainer may also use GitHub's reporting tools.
