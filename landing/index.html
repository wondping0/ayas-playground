A LITTLE CARE BEHIND THE CURIOSITY

Playful puzzles.
Thoughtful checks.

01

A solve that has been checked.

The author's solver runs in an isolated sandbox. A passing result shows it recovered the flag in that run; it doesn't promise the absence of unintended solutions.

02

A fresh flag, when the format allows.

Re-flaggable bundles get an order-specific flag. Static bundles keep the same fixed flag for every buyer. Fresh flags offer traceability, not copy protection.

03

Your playground, your setup.

Aya supplies the artifact. Event hosting, live challenge instances, and scoreboards stay with your own CTF platform.

GOOD QUESTIONS

Before you jump in.

Curiosity is always welcome.

Is the marketplace open?+

The marketplace is in development. This page introduces the project and includes a small number warm-up; it has no checkout, account creation, or payment collection.

Are these all number puzzles?+

The number theme celebrates the curiosity behind cryptography. The marketplace focuses on cryptography CTF challenges, from RSA weaknesses to keystream reuse. The small sequence puzzle above is just a warm-up.

What comes in a challenge bundle?+

A challenge manifest, the player handout, the author's solver, and a writeup. For generate-mode challenges, generator source is included only with an unlimited licence.

Can I run the project without Docker?+

Yes, for UI development. The app can boot with an in-memory store and a stub runner. The stub never passes verification; actual verification requires the real sandbox runner.
